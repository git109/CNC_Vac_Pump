Using the ST Morpho headers — read this. The Morpho connectors (CN7, CN10) bring out every MCU pin, so they're less foolproof than the Arduino header. Electrically it's the same pin either way — nothing in the firmware/overlay changes — but physically:

• Count carefully. Each connector is 2×19 = 38 pins: odd column (1,3,5…) one side, even (2,4,6…) the other. Pin 1 is silk-marked. Analog + power (A0–A5, 5 V, 3V3) are on CN7; digital D0–D15 are on CN10.

• Avoid the debug/system pins the Arduino header hid: PA13/PA14 (SWD), NRST, PH3/BOOT0 — wiring to these can break flashing. And leave PA2/PA3 (CN10-35/37, D1/D0) alone — they're the ST-LINK console VCP.

• Solder-bridge-shared pads: PA7 (CN10-15, shares w/ PA10), PA4 (CN10-17, shares w/ PB10), PA1 (CN7-32, shares w/ PB6). The board defaults to the pin we use — don't move the solder bridges.

Power the LCD from 3V3, not 5V. The module's VCC must match the 3.3 V signal level — a 5 V-powered panel driven by 3.3 V logic typically shows blank/garbled.

No MISO. The panel is write-only (only SDA data-in), so the SPI bus is SCK + MOSI only and CS is a GPIO.

BLK is active-high, on by default (internal pull-up to 3.3 V). Firmware drives PA1 high to enable it; grounding BLK suspends the backlight.

Vacuum sensor front-end (analog)

NXP MPXV4115V (5 V ratiometric part) → resistor divider → the 3.3 V ADC. The divider both scales the 0.2–4.6 V output into range and protects the ADC pin (never wire the raw sensor output to the MCU).

Divider math: ratio = 10k/(5k+10k) = 0.667 → max 4.6 V × 0.667 = 3.07 V (93% of the 3.3 V full scale, safely under the 3.6 V pin max). Source impedance = 5k∥10k = 3.3 kΩ, handled by the 47.5-cycle ADC sample time in the overlay.

Calibration: the firmware's raw→inHg map still uses the ESP32's 4090 full scale — retune to the real min/max counts on the WB55 (~3810 at 3.07 V). See the TODO(calibrate…) in src/main.c.

Notes

  • Encoder A/B/SW use internal pull-ups — idle high, each switches to GND.
  • Relays are active-low (matches the firmware's On = 0 convention).
  • Console + flashing share the single ST-LINK USB (USART1 VCP) — no wiring needed.
